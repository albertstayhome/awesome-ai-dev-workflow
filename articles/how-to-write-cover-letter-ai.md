# How to Use AI to Write a Cover Letter That Actually Gets Interviews (100% Free & Private)

If you are job hunting in 2026, you already know how exhausting the process is. You spend hours tailoring your resume, filling out repetitive Workday portals, and then—just when you think you're done—the application demands a customized cover letter.

Writing a unique cover letter for every single application is soul-crushing. Most applicants skip it, or they use a generic template that hiring managers can spot from a mile away.

## The AI Trap

Naturally, many job seekers have turned to AI (like ChatGPT or Claude) to write their cover letters. The typical workflow involves pasting your resume and the job description into the chatbot and asking for a letter.

However, there are two massive problems with this approach:
1. **Privacy Risks:** When you paste your resume into a public AI chatbot, you are surrendering your personal data (phone number, address, work history) to their training models.
2. **Generic Output:** Unless you use a very specific prompt, the AI will generate a robotic, bloated letter that starts with "I am thrilled to apply for..."

## Introducing AI Cover Letter Pro

To solve both the privacy and quality issues, independent developers created **[AI Cover Letter Pro](https://albertstayhome.github.io/ai-cover-letter-web/)**.

This is a 100% free, 100% private web application designed specifically to generate world-class, highly tailored cover letters.

### Why it is better than ChatGPT:

* **Client-Side Privacy:** The application runs entirely in your local browser. Your resume and the job description are **never sent to a backend server**. 
* **Bring Your Own Key:** Instead of charging you a monthly subscription, the tool connects directly to Google using your personal (and free) Gemini API key. You maintain total control over your data.
* **Expert Prompting:** Under the hood, the app uses a heavily engineered prompt designed by career coaches. It ensures the AI doesn't hallucinate experience you don't have, and formats the letter perfectly.

### How to use it in 3 steps

1. **Get a Key:** Head over to Google AI Studio and generate a free API key.
2. **Paste Your Info:** Go to **[AI Cover Letter Pro](https://albertstayhome.github.io/ai-cover-letter-web/)** and paste your API key, your resume text, and the job description.
3. **Generate:** Click the button. In less than 5 seconds, a highly customized, compelling cover letter will be generated on your screen.

You can instantly copy it to your clipboard and submit your application.
